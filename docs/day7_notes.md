# Day 7 - Exploratory Data Analysis

## Objective

Explore the cleaned financial dataset using descriptive statistics,
company-level comparisons, trends, and correlation analysis.

## Analysis Performed

- Dataset size analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Average dividend per share by company
- Average dividend yield by company
- Year-wise dividend trends
- Year-wise stock price trends
- Year-wise dividend yield trends
- Stock price vs dividend comparison
- Correlation analysis
- Company ranking by dividend yield
- Company ranking by dividend per share

## Important Variables

- Year-end stock price
- Dividend per share
- Dividend yield

## Key Questions

1. Which companies have higher average dividend yields?
2. How have dividends changed over time?
3. How have stock prices changed over time?
4. Is there a relationship between stock price and dividend?
5. How strongly are price, dividend and dividend yield correlated?
