# Dividend Puzzle Analyzer - Data Dictionary

## Company Information

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| company | Company name |
| ticker | Stock ticker |
| year | Financial year |

## Dividend Variables

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| dividend_per_share | Dividend paid per share |
| dividend_yield | Dividend relative to stock price |
| payout_ratio | Percentage of earnings paid as dividends |

## Profitability Variables

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| revenue | Company's total revenue |
| net_income | Company's profit |
| eps | Earnings per share |
| roe | Return on equity |

## Financial Variables

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| total_assets | Total company assets |
| total_debt | Total debt |
| cash | Cash and cash equivalents |

## Market Variables

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| stock_price | Stock price |
| market_cap | Market capitalization |

## Analysis Variables

These variables will later be used to investigate the Dividend Puzzle:

- dividend stability
- dividend growth
- earnings growth
- stock return
- profitability
- leverage
- company size
