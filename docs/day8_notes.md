# Day 8 - Dividend Growth and Stock Return Analysis

## Objective

Investigate whether dividend growth is associated with stock performance.

## New Variables

- Dividend Growth
- Price Growth
- Annual Stock Return
- Total Return

## Dividend Growth

Dividend Growth measures the percentage change in dividend per share
from the previous year.

## Annual Stock Return

Annual Stock Return is calculated using the year-over-year change
in year-end stock price.

## Simplified Total Return

Total Return = Annual Stock Return + Dividend Yield

This is a simplified annual measure based on our available data.

## Analysis Performed

- Average dividend growth by company
- Average annual stock return by company
- Average total return by company
- Dividend growth vs stock return correlation
- Dividend growth vs stock return scatter plot
- Company comparisons

## Research Question

Do companies with higher dividend growth also provide higher
stock returns?

## Important Note

Correlation does not prove causation.

The analysis is exploratory and does not yet provide a final
explanation of the Dividend Puzzle.
