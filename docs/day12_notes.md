# Day 12 - Financial Fundamentals

## Objective

Prepare financial fundamental variables that can help explain
the relationship between dividend policy, company characteristics
and stock performance.

## Financial Variables

- Revenue
- Net Income
- EPS
- Market Capitalization
- Total Debt
- Total Assets
- Shares Outstanding

## Financial Ratios

### Payout Ratio

Payout Ratio measures the proportion of earnings distributed
as dividends.

Payout Ratio = Dividend Per Share / EPS × 100

### Profit Margin

Profit Margin measures the percentage of revenue converted into
net income.

Profit Margin = Net Income / Revenue × 100

### ROA

Return on Assets measures profitability relative to total assets.

ROA = Net Income / Total Assets × 100

### ROE

Return on Equity measures profitability relative to shareholders'
equity.

Equity = Total Assets - Total Debt

ROE = Net Income / Equity × 100

## Important Rule

Financial values must come from real data sources. Random or
manually invented financial values must not be used.

## Research Importance

Financial fundamentals can act as explanatory/control variables
when investigating why dividend-paying companies may have different
valuation or return characteristics.
