# Day 22 – Signaling Theory Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 22 is to test whether dividend-related variables provide evidence consistent with Dividend Signaling Theory.

## Signaling Theory

Signaling Theory suggests that companies may use dividend payments and changes in dividends to communicate information about their financial condition and future prospects to investors.

## Variables Used

- Dividend Yield
- Dividend Growth
- Dividend Consistency
- Annual Stock Return
- Total Return
- Return Volatility

## Analysis Performed

1. Created a signaling-theory-specific dataset.
2. Removed rows with missing values in required variables.
3. Calculated correlation coefficients.
4. Analyzed dividend growth against total return.
5. Analyzed dividend consistency against total return.
6. Analyzed dividend yield against total return.
7. Analyzed dividend growth against annual stock return.

## Results

The correlation results are stored in:

`datasets/processed/day22_signaling_results.csv`

## Conclusion

The observed relationships will be evaluated to determine whether the data provides evidence consistent with Dividend Signaling Theory.

Correlation alone does not establish causation. Therefore, the results should be interpreted as empirical evidence supporting or contradicting the theory rather than definitive proof.
