# Day 23 – Agency Cost Theory Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 23 is to examine whether dividend-related variables provide empirical evidence consistent with Agency Cost Theory.

## Agency Cost Theory

Agency Cost Theory suggests that dividend payments can reduce conflicts between managers and shareholders by distributing excess cash to shareholders.

## Variables Used

- Dividend Yield
- Dividend Growth
- Dividend Consistency
- Profit Margin
- Return on Assets (ROA)
- Total Return

## Analysis Performed

1. Created an Agency Cost Theory dataset.
2. Checked missing values.
3. Removed incomplete observations.
4. Calculated correlation coefficients.
5. Analyzed Dividend Yield vs Profit Margin.
6. Analyzed Dividend Yield vs ROA.
7. Analyzed Dividend Yield vs Total Return.
8. Analyzed Dividend Growth vs Profit Margin.
9. Analyzed Dividend Growth vs ROA.

## Results

The correlation results are stored in:

`datasets/processed/day23_agency_results.csv`

## Conclusion

The observed relationships will be evaluated to determine whether the data provides evidence consistent with Agency Cost Theory.

Correlation represents association and does not establish causation. Therefore, the results should be interpreted as empirical evidence rather than definitive proof of the theory.
